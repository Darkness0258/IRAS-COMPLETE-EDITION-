              Who decides

              IRAS can do a lot, but being able to is not the same as being allowed to. Every action has a level, and the screen always shows it with an icon and a word.

              • Safe
                Reading and everyday actionsIRAS just does it and logs it. Examples: read your calendar, play music.
              • System action
                Changes on your PCIRAS asks first, in amber. Examples: install software, send a message.
              • Critical
                Can’t be undoneIRAS asks in red and wants your voice within 9 seconds. Examples: delete files.
