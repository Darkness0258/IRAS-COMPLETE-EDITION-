How this stays safe

  • {{i:lock-keyhole}}
    You start it, you see itNothing reaches your PC without a session you started. A badge in the top bar shows it, and End session stops it at once.
  • {{i:shield-check}}
    Three limits, not oneThe session level, your server's limit and the PC's own rules all apply. The lowest one wins.
  • {{i:eye-off}}
    Nothing stays on screenPictures and results from your PC are cleared when you leave this screen or end the session.
